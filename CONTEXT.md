# Choir Naming Election

This context describes the one-time collective decision through which the choir selects its name from anonymously submitted suggestions.

## Language

**Name Election**:
The complete, one-time process through which the choir selects its name.
_Avoid_: Poll, campaign

**Suggestion**:
A proposed choir name together with the anonymous motivation supplied for it.
_Avoid_: Result, response, contender

**Participant**:
A named choir member who is eligible to take part in the Name Election once.
_Avoid_: User, voter

**Invitation**:
A participant-specific bearer link that identifies its holder as that Participant. The Name Election relies on choir members not using one another's Invitations.
_Avoid_: Account, login

**Approval Round**:
The first stage, in which Participants give every revealed Suggestion either Yay or Nay approval.
_Avoid_: Initial poll, stage one

**Approval Score**:
The share of submitted responses to a Suggestion that are Yay. Participants who did not answer that Suggestion are not included in its score.
_Avoid_: Percentage of all Participants

**Reveal Frontier**:
The latest Suggestion made available by the Administrator. Participants may catch up on earlier Suggestions but cannot access Suggestions beyond this point.
_Avoid_: Current slide, current index

**Presentation Position**:
The Suggestion currently shown on the shared presentation display. It may move backward or forward among revealed Suggestions without changing the Reveal Frontier.
_Avoid_: Reveal Frontier, current index

**Finalist**:
A Suggestion deliberately selected by the Administrator for the Final Vote after reviewing Approval Round totals.
_Avoid_: Top contender

**Final Vote**:
The stage in which each Participant distributes a fixed allowance of votes among the Finalists.
_Avoid_: Stage two, favorite vote

**Vote Token**:
One indivisible unit of a Participant's Final Vote allowance. Vote Tokens may be stacked on one Finalist or split among several Finalists.
_Avoid_: Point, preference

**Runoff**:
An additional vote among tied leading Finalists used to determine the winner.
_Avoid_: Tiebreaker

**Ballot**:
A Participant's evolving set of choices within one voting round. A Ballot remains editable until that round closes.
_Avoid_: Submission

**Result Reveal**:
The Administrator-triggered publication and celebration of a closed vote's outcome.
_Avoid_: Vote close

**Winner**:
The leading Finalist when the Name Election is completed. Multiple Finalists may be declared joint Winners after repeated tied Runoffs.
_Avoid_: Selected option

**Administrator**:
The trusted session leader who manages Participants, controls progression, selects Finalists, and opens and closes voting stages.
_Avoid_: Host, moderator
